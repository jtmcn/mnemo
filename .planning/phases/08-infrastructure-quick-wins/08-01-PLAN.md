<objective>
Migrate ChromaDB from L2 to cosine distance and expose real similarity scores.

Purpose: L2 distance is less interpretable for similarity search. Cosine distance enables meaningful 0-1 relevance scores and better ranking for semantic search. The migration preserves existing embeddings (no re-embedding needed).

Output: Migration script, updated VectorStore, CLI command, cosine similarity scores in search results.
</objective>
